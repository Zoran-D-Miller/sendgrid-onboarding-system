Grow funded accounts without compliance surprises

In financial services, a partner who makes the wrong claim about your rates is a regulatory problem, not just a brand one. The banks that scale partnerships review partner content before it goes live and pay on funded accounts, not sign-ups.

Retail and ecommerce

Pay more for new customers, less for repeat orders

Retail programs leak margin when a coupon site gets full commission on a customer who was already at checkout. Brands that grow profitably split commission by new versus returning customer and give creators the bigger share.

return on partner spend for an apparel brand after moving to new-customer commission rates and adding 40 creators to its program.
